02ISC2 Certified in Cybersecurity (CC)ISC2 • Certified+

The ISC2 Certified in Cybersecurity (CC) is an entry-level certification that validates foundational cybersecurity knowledge. It is designed for people beginning or building toward cybersecurity work and does not require prior professional experience.

Knowledge areas
  • Security principles and governance
  • Identity and access management (IAM)
  • Networking and cloud security concepts
  • Security operations and incident response

What it demonstrates: a verified foundation in core security concepts and commitment to continued learning. It supports an entry-level pathway, alongside practical experience and further development.
